Telugu for Kids — Simple Lessons

This page is designed for children and parents who want to learn Telugu in a simple and fun way.

1. Start with the Telugu Alphabet

Kids should first learn how the letters look and sound.

Learn the Telugu Alphabet →

2. Basic Words for Kids

  • అమ్మ — Mother
  • నాన్న — Father
  • బొమ్మ — Toy
  • పిల్లి — Cat
  • కుక్క — Dog
  • పండు — Fruit
  • పాలు — Milk

3. Simple Telugu Sentences for Kids

  • నేను బొమ్మతో ఆడుతున్నాను — I am playing with a toy
  • నాకు పాలు ఇష్టం — I like milk
  • ఇది నా ఇల్లు — This is my house

More sentences: 100 Basic Telugu Sentences

4. Tips for Parents

  • Speak simple Telugu at home daily.
  • Show pictures and say the Telugu word.
  • Repeat the same words and sentences often.
  • Use our vocabulary categories to teach by topic.
