Telugu Pronunciation Guide (English to Telugu)

Telugu is a phonetic language. Once you learn the sounds of each letter, you can pronounce most words correctly.

1. Vowels (Achchulu)

Some basic vowels with English-like sounds:

  • అ — a (as in “ago”)
  • ఆ — aa (as in “father”)
  • ఇ — i (as in “ink”)
  • ఈ — ee (as in “see”)
  • ఉ — u (as in “put”)
  • ఊ — oo (as in “food”)
  • ఎ — e (as in “pen”)
  • ఏ — ee (as in “cake” but longer)
  • ఒ — o (as in “hot”)
  • ఓ — o (as in “go”)

2. Consonants (Hallulu)

Some common consonants:

  • క — ka
  • గ — ga
  • చ — cha
  • జ — ja
  • ట — ta (retroflex)
  • డ — da (retroflex)
  • త — tha (dental)
  • ద — dha (dental)
  • ప — pa
  • బ — ba
  • మ — ma
  • న — na

3. Common Words with Pronunciation

  • అమ్మ (amma) — Mother
  • నాన్న (naanna) — Father
  • ఇల్లు (illu) — House
  • నీళ్ళు (neellu) — Water
  • పుస్తకం (pustakam) — Book

4. Tips to Improve Pronunciation

  • Learn the Telugu alphabet first.
  • Listen to Telugu audio and repeat aloud.
  • Practice reading simple Telugu sentences.
